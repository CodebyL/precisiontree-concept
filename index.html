What we do

Four kinds of work, one careful crew.

Read it like the rings of a stump: the core job first, then everything around it.

  1. 1

    Tree removal

    Whole trees taken down in sections, so nothing drops where it should not. We talk through the plan with you before the first cut, and the wood and brush leave with us.

    Dead, leaning or simply in the way
  2. 2

    Pruning

    Thinning, raising and clearing limbs off the roof, the wires and the driveway. Good pruning keeps a tree healthy and lets light back into the yard.

    Keep the tree, lose the problem limbs
  3. 3

    Work in tight spaces

    Narrow side yards, trees hugging the house, branches over a fence. Limbs come down on ropes, piece by piece, instead of falling.

    What our customers notice most
  4. 4

    Yard cleanup

    Branches, brush and storm leftovers gathered and hauled away, so the yard is usable again by the end of the day.

    After storms or before the season

Tight spaces

Close to the house is our kind of job.

Plenty of Long Island trees were planted when the house was new and the yard was empty. Now they lean over roofs, sheds and pools. Those are the jobs that need patience and a plan, and that is where Precision Tree earns its name.
